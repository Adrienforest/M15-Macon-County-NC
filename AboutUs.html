About Macon County, NC

Macon County is dedicated to providing transparent, inclusive, and effective government services to our residents, visitors, and businesses. Located in the scenic Appalachian Mountains, we are proud of our heritage and community values.

Our Mission

To serve the public with integrity and efficiency, while preserving the natural beauty and historical significance of our region.

Community

We value strong community connections and civic engagement.

Responsibility

Our decisions are made with accountability and sustainability in mind.

Service

Committed to delivering exceptional services that meet public needs.

Our History

Founded in 1828, Macon County has a rich legacy of mountain culture, economic development, and civic progress. From early agriculture to modern tourism and technology, we continue to grow while respecting our roots.
