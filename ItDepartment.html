Information Technology Department

Supporting Macon County’s systems, services, and security with professionalism and innovation.

Our Role

The IT Department provides technology leadership and support for Macon County government. We ensure the secure, efficient, and accessible use of technology to better serve the public and county employees.

Cybersecurity

Protecting county systems and data with robust security practices and monitoring.

Network Management

Maintaining secure and reliable network infrastructure across all departments.

Technical Support

Providing prompt support to staff, ensuring continuity and efficiency in county operations.

Cloud Services

Implementing cloud solutions for enhanced collaboration, storage, and disaster recovery.
